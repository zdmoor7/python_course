01 — My Story

From Medical Writing
to Sales Engineering

I spent several years as a medical writer for large enterprise pharma companies, shaping content strategy and go-to-market paths for new drugs and treatments. The role involved constant collaboration with subject matter experts and senior stakeholders—and I loved that side of it.

But over time I realized the work had become too solitary, and I wanted something that sat at the intersection of interpersonal communication and technical storytelling.

Sales Engineering is that role. In the months since deciding to make the transition, I've networked with over 40 Sales Engineers, completed a Demo2Win bootcamp, and built a suite of AI-powered tools from scratch—including this site. I'm beyond excited to bring these skills into a real role.
